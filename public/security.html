Security & Data Protection

Business information deserves careful handling.

KONARA is designed around layered safeguards, authenticated access, organisation boundaries and controlled operational processes. Security is treated as an ongoing responsibility rather than a one-time feature.

No online service can responsibly promise absolute security. KONARA therefore focuses on reducing risk through multiple technical and organisational controls, careful access management and continuous review.
01 · Access

Authenticated private access

Private KONARA areas require authenticated sessions. Customer operations are designed around organisation membership and role-aware permissions rather than public access.

02 · Isolation

Organisation boundaries

Customer information is associated with an organisation and server-side controls are used to keep one organisation's data separated from another organisation's authorised workspace.

03 · Transport

Protected web connections

Production traffic is served over HTTPS. KONARA also applies restrictive browser security controls including HSTS, content security policies, framing protection and content-type safeguards.

04 · Sensitive information

Minimise unnecessary exposure

Passwords, authentication tokens, provider secrets and private credentials are not intended to appear in normal customer interfaces or ordinary administrative views.

05 · Changes

Controlled production updates

KONARA separates application releases from high-risk data operations. Database migrations and destructive operations require additional review rather than being treated as routine interface changes.

06 · Payments

Payment information

KONARA's application does not need to store raw payment-card details in its ordinary customer database. Payment processing is handled through the configured specialist payment provider.

07 · Administration

Restricted platform operations

Administrative capabilities are separated from ordinary customer access. Important account, access and operational changes are designed to be controlled and auditable.

08 · Incidents

Security response

KONARA maintains an internal process for investigating suspected security incidents, containing affected access, preserving relevant evidence, recovering service and reviewing the underlying cause.

Responsible handling

Security claims should match the system that actually exists.

KONARA avoids promising that any system is impossible to compromise. Public statements are intended to describe safeguards that have been implemented or verified. Privacy obligations, retention requirements, data-processing terms and regulatory responsibilities are maintained separately in KONARA's legal documentation.

If you believe you have identified a security issue, contact KONARA privately through the official contact channel and identify the message as a security matter. Please do not publish sensitive customer information or credentials.
